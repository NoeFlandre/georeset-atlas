# GeoReSeT Atlas notices

This notice records attribution and source lineage for the components included in the Atlas. It does not add a license grant.

## OpenStreetMap

© OpenStreetMap contributors. OSM-derived geometry and tags are identified by the pinned source cards as ODbL 1.0 material. See the [OpenStreetMap copyright page](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and the [ODbL 1.0 legal text](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Preserve the source notices and comply with the terms that apply to those components.

## Wikidata and Wikimedia text

The pinned Wiki source identifies Wikidata structured facts as CC0 1.0 Universal and Wikipedia/Wikivoyage text under CC BY-SA terms. Keep each source document's original license and attribution fields. The full assembled export has not yet been read back to verify those fields.

Official texts: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en), and [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode).

## Website text

Website text and sentence copies remain subject to the original sites' terms. The pinned Website source card says this text is not covered by ODbL. No general redistribution permission is recorded. Source URLs are retained for attribution and traceability. Atlas does not grant a license to the Website text.

Public release of Website text remains blocked until source-site redistribution rights are documented and approved. The local assembly may preserve all three sources while that gate remains open.
